College Representative, College of Agricultural Science, Student Council

Landmark University, Nigeria · July 2024 to July 2025

Most often, agriculture students at Landmark University do not practise agriculture after graduating, partly because they don’t know which companies are hiring. I led my team to organise the Agro Green Festival, bringing agricultural companies and sponsors onto campus, and over 80% of students surveyed rated it impactful or highly impactful.

I also noticed that students caught without sanitary products had to walk back to the hostel, so I stocked pads in the student office, and I introduced snacks to keep students going through exam reading periods. About 70% of students surveyed rated the welfare initiatives impactful or highly impactful.

I kept Farmers’ Market Day and College Week running, and I mobilised students to support the college at the Chancellor’s Cup, where it placed 2nd. Over 90% rated both Farmers’ Market Day and the Chancellor’s Cup impactful or highly impactful.

The feedback also showed where I can improve: communication with students, planning events earlier, and academic support, which just over half rated impactful. I’m working on these in my current role as a student ambassador at Loughborough University London.
